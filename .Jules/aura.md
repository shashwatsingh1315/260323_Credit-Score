## 2024-10-24 - [Arbitrary Bracket Notation Values] **Learning:** The legacy UI heavily uses arbitrary bracket pixel values (e.g., `text-[11px]`, `w-[200px]`, `w-[50px]`, `max-h-[400px]`) which completely breaks out of the standard Base-4/Base-8 scaling and typography systems. This makes the UI unscalable and visually inconsistent. **Action:** Introduced `text-micro` for `11px` in `tailwind.config.js` and will systematically strip out all bracket values, replacing them with standardized Tailwind tokens (`w-48`, `text-tiny`, `min-h-32`, etc.).
