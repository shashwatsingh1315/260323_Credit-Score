How should we handle Overdue Information for Grandfathered Cases?

The native Collections module calculates overdue days dynamically based on tranches and outstanding amounts. How much detail should we require for grandfathered imports?

A

Simple Import (Recommended)

Import just the Total Outstanding Amount and the Original Due Date. The system will automatically create a single "consolidated" tranche in the background. It's much easier for RMs to compile this data.

Fields: Name, ID, RM, Remarks, Outstanding Amount, Due Date
B

Full Tranche Parity

Require the import to break down the outstanding amount into its original tranches (Tranche 1 amount/date, Tranche 2 amount/date, etc.). Harder to compile, but provides identical granularity to native cases.

Fields: Name, ID, RM, Remarks, Tranche 1 (Amt/Date), Tranche 2 (Amt/Date)...
